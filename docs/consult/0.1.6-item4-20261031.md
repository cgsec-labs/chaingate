## Verdict

Move item 4 to the next release and cut the 0.1.6 candidate now. The owner's recommendation stands as given.

Why: the action is a separate interface with its own test surface and its own CI matrix, nothing in 0.1.6 depends on it, and
0.1.6 is the release that makes the tool installable by a stranger (no 1 GB download, hourly advisories, every download walked).
Holding that for a wrapper around `chaingate check` would delay the thing the wrapper needs to exist first: a published seed and a
feed that a CI job can fetch.
